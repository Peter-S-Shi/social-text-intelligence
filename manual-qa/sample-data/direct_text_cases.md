# Direct Analysis Cases

All text below is synthetic English material. Copy only the text in each
blockquote into **Direct analysis**. The review focus is not a prescribed
ground-truth label.

## D01 — Clear positive

> The setup guide was easy to follow, and the result worked on my first try.

Review focus: clear favorable wording and a straightforward positive signal.

## D02 — Clear negative

> The update deleted my draft and left me frustrated with the entire process.

Review focus: explicit negative wording with a likely anger or annoyance signal.

## D03 — Neutral information

> The maintenance window begins at 02:00 UTC and is scheduled to last thirty minutes.

Review focus: factual language with no strong evaluative claim.

## D04 — Mixed experience

> The response was quick and courteous, but the original problem is still unresolved.

Review focus: positive service language and a negative unresolved outcome in one
record.

## D05 — Negation

> I am not unhappy with the change, although I still need time to understand it.

Review focus: whether negation is handled plausibly without overstating
certainty.

## D06 — Polite criticism

> Thank you for the explanation; the instructions could still be much clearer for a first-time user.

Review focus: courteous wording combined with constructive dissatisfaction.

## D07 — Sarcasm-like wording

> Great, another delay just when I finally had time to finish the task.

Review focus: a known limitation where surface-positive wording may conflict
with implied negative meaning.

## D08 — Multiple emotions

> I am relieved the file was recovered, surprised it happened at all, and worried it may happen again.

Review focus: multiple independent emotion activations and clear threshold
semantics.

## D09 — Low-emotion factual report

> The page loaded after two retries and displayed the expected confirmation number.

Review focus: descriptive wording that should not be turned into a psychological
conclusion.

## D10 — Context-dependent ambiguity

> That was exactly what I expected.

Review focus: insufficient context, confidence transparency, and the need for
human judgment.
