# Round 3 reference set

**Sources.** The material came from two sites:

- onepagelove.com: 650 entries scanned across the SaaS, App, Digital Product,
  Minimal, Informational, Modular, Grid, Typographic, Editorial, Corporate,
  Finance, Long-form Journalism, shadcn/ui, and selected section categories.
- motionsites.ai: the Apps category.

**How they were filtered.**

1. A keyword filter on each site's own description cut the scan to 142
   entries. It kept tools, data, local/offline, reports, editorial, and
   desktop apps, and removed weddings, games, 3D, and similar.
2. Each remaining candidate was reviewed visually, from its first screen and,
   for the finalists, from the full-page capture cut into slices.

**What a reference is used for.** Only for structure, typography, or a
specific component. No layout is copied whole, and nothing from V1's visual
language is reused.

## Looked at and rejected for this product

| Reference | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| The "Behind the Hashtag" series (thedress, heforshe, breaktheinternet, and others) | On-topic social-media analysis, but onepagelove keeps only hero shots. The structure cannot be studied. |
| owomaniya (diversity report) | Strong editorial, but its loud campaign styling fights a calm review tool. |
| Weppy, Shiori, TinyShots, ScreenTune | Good native-app marketing; little transferable for data or review screens. |
| motionsites F1 Racing Hub, Forecast Center, Reading Library | Photo- and 3D-driven. Not reproducible in Qt Widgets, and wrong in tone. |
| Most of motionsites (heroes, video backgrounds) | Marketing effects with no desktop-tool equivalent. |
